# Outreach contract

How every note job-outreach sends is shaped. Pasted verbatim as `CONTRACT` to
`job-humanize`, followed by the `### Number firewall`, `### Credit`, and
`### Surface` subsections of the `## Voice law` in
`job-apply/references/contracts/contract-prose.md`, and by its
`## Forbidden claims`. Where those name slot 2, read Fit. The Subject and
every line marked (verbatim) are verbatim: rewrite nothing on them.

## Sources

The `## Sources` of `job-apply/references/contracts/contract-prose.md`, plus:

- the dossier `company`, `title`, and `## The role`;
- one fact about the company's product or problem, printed by the ad or by a
  company page read this run;
- `data/basics.yaml` `name` and `url.href`;
- the LinkedIn and GitHub URLs in `data/profiles.yaml`.

Cut any sentence that traces back to none of these.

**Carrying story**: the `ready` or `needs-numbers` story whose `covers`
overlaps the ad most. No such story → the role in `data/experiences.yml` that
overlaps the ad most, stated in one sentence with no number.

## first

- Subject: `{title} at {company}`, both exactly as the dossier prints them,
  minus a trailing location or work-model tag on the title such as
  `| REMOTE` or `- Remote`. No `Re:`, no emoji.
- Body, in this order:
  1. (verbatim) `Hi {first name},`. A role mailbox, or an address with no
     name the page prints → `Hi {company} team,`.
  2. Fit: one sentence that opens on their need, not on `I`, states the fit,
     and says the application is in.
  3. Proof: the carrying story's claim. An outcome number only where the
     number firewall allows it.
  4. Specific: one sentence on their product or problem, from the company
     fact in Sources.
  5. Ask: one sentence asking for a 15-minute call, or for the right person
     to talk to.
  6. (verbatim) Links line: LinkedIn · GitHub · site, the filled-in ones, as
     bare URLs.
  7. (verbatim) `{name}`
- Lines 2–5 hold at most 120 words together.

## follow-up-1

Same greeting and name lines (verbatim). Between them, at most 50 words: one
fact the first note did not carry, either a second story's claim or a
requirement the dossier's `## The role` lists that a Source answers. Then the
ask once more.

## follow-up-2

Same greeting and name lines (verbatim). Between them, at most 40 words: say
this is the last note and ask once more. No new claim.

## multiplier

Same greeting and name lines (verbatim). Between them, at most 60 words: one
clause that accepts the decision, then one ask: whether they know another
team or company hiring for this kind of role, plus one sentence on what fits.
Never argue with the decision or ask them to reconsider.

## Never

- Terms: geography, work authorization, salary
- A second ask
- Urgency borrowed from other hiring processes
- A referral, connection, or meeting that is not on record
- An attachment the message does not carry
- Opening with "follow up" or "just checking in"
- Any `never_say` claim
