The service

Resume Check is a free MCP server that scans resume files for parsing risks and computes rubric scores. It is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, and may change or stop at any time.

Acceptable use

Send only files you have the right to share, and links that point to a resume file. Do not use the service to probe networks, overload it, get around its limits, or process other people's documents without their permission.

No advice

Scores and findings are guidance from a designed rubric. They are not an employer's applicant tracking score and do not predict hiring outcomes. You remain responsible for your resume and applications.
